What to include

If you're asking about an event, the more you can tell us up front, the faster we can give you a real answer:

The date
And a rough start and finish time if you know them.
The location
Venue and city. We play across DC, Maryland, Virginia and Pennsylvania.
The size of band
Anywhere from a trio to an octet—or say you're not sure and we'll suggest one. Compare the configurations.
The kind of event
A ceremony, a dance floor and a cocktail hour all want different things from a band. See what we play for.

Elsewhere

We read everything that comes in. If you're asking about a date, mention it up front—it saves a round trip.

We're based in Washington, DC and play throughout the District, Maryland, Virginia and Pennsylvania. Everywhere from the Kennedy Center and the Woodrow Wilson House to a street corner in Takoma Park.
