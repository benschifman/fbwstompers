Weddings · DC, Maryland & Virginia

A live jazz band for your wedding

We bring a vintage flair to weddings across DC, Maryland and Virginia—the ceremony, the cocktail hour, the reception, and any other part of the day that wants live music.

Check your date Hear us play

Through the whole day, or just part of it

Most couples book us for one or two parts of the day. Some book us for all of it.

The ceremony. We play the standards you'd expect—Pachelbel's Canon, Here Comes the Bride—and we're glad to learn something else if you have a piece in mind. Processional, recessional, and the quiet stretches in between.

The cocktail hour. This is where a small band earns its keep: warm, unobtrusive, and quiet enough that your guests can actually hear each other.

The first dance. Tell us the song. If it isn't already in the book we'll learn it, and we'll arrange it to suit the size of band you've booked.

The dancing. Once the floor opens we play the jazz dance numbers of the era—the music that was written to be danced to in the first place.

What comes with the band

More than just the music

A PA system
We bring our own sound. You don't need to hire one separately or work out who's providing it.
An MC
We can announce the different parts of the evening—the entrance, the first dance, the toasts—so nobody's left wondering what happens next.
A band sized to the room
An acoustic trio for an intimate ceremony, or the full octet when the dance floor fills up. Compare the configurations.
Songs you've asked for
Beyond the 72 standards already in the book, we'll learn what you want for the moments that matter.
