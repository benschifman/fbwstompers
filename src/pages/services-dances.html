A live jazz band for dancers

The jazz music of the 1920s was dance music, and we love playing it for dancers—live, not off a playlist, across DC, Maryland and Virginia.

Music written to be danced to

This isn't music we adapted for dancing—it's what it was written for in the first place.

Playing it live for a full floor is a different job than playing for people seated at dinner, and it's the part of the work we like best.

We've partnered with dance teachers to offer lessons, demonstrations, and even burlesque alongside the band, for organizers who want the floor to be part of the evening rather than an afterthought.

We also ran our own Speakeasy Night events in 2018 and 2019, and we'd like to bring them back.
