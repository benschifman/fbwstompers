Learn about the Stompers

Stompin' since 2015

The Foggy Bottom Whomp-Stompers formed in 2015 at a flashband event playing music in the public domain—which is to say, anything written before about 1925.

Since then we've been stomping on like the Ship of Theseus, with members coming and going, but always keeping the 1920s music flowing.

We've played private parties and public events throughout the District, Virginia, Maryland, and even the commonwealth of Pennsylvania. We've played everywhere from the Kennedy Center, the Woodrow Wilson House and the Torpedo Factory Art Center to a humble street corner.

We play in several different sized ensembles, from an acoustic core trio for an intimate ceremony to the full octet when the dance floor demands it. You can compare the configurations, browse what we play, or see the kinds of events we take on.
