Sized to the room, not the other way around

A house party isn't a wedding hall, and the band shouldn't be sized like one.

The theme. If you're throwing a 1920s themed birthday or a straight-up vintage party, that's exactly the sound we play—live 1920s and 1930s jazz, not a playlist standing in for it.

The space. A living room or a small event space calls for something different than a ballroom. We can bring a big or small band suitable for the size of your event, from an intimate trio up to the full octet.

The sound. We bring our own PA. A private home or a smaller venue doesn't need to supply one, and you don't need to coordinate rentals with anyone else.
