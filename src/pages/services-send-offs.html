What a second line is

In New Orleans tradition, a jazz funeral moves the music from something slow and solemn on the way to the service into something brighter afterward—a second line, played to honor a life while celebrating it. Not every service calls for that arc, and not every family wants a brass band. We're glad to talk through what would suit the service, whether that's a single quiet piece or something closer to the full tradition.

We've been playing 1920s and 1930s jazz since 2015, and we bring that same music—and the same care—to services and celebrations of life across DC, Maryland, Virginia and Pennsylvania.
