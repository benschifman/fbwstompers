Built for the crowd, not just the room

Festival audiences are moving—people walk up, listen for a minute, and move on. Our set is built for that.

An act that works outdoors. We bring our own PA system, so there's no separate sound rental to coordinate and no dependence on house equipment that may or may not exist on a festival field.

A set that catches a walk-up crowd. The same 1920s and 1930s jazz that's worked for us on street corners has worked at the Washington Folk Festival and the Torpedo Factory Art Center—music written to be heard in passing, not just seated for.

A lineup sized to your stage and your budget. A 3-piece trio for a small program stage or a tight budget, up to the full 8-piece octet for a main stage. Compare the configurations.
